Installing District Lookup

1. Drag "District Lookup" onto the Applications folder in this window.
2. Open it from your Applications folder.

The first time, macOS says it can't check District Lookup for malicious
software, because the app isn't signed with a paid Apple developer account.
To open it anyway:

  macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later:
    Click Done. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down to
    the message about District Lookup, and click Open Anyway. Confirm with
    your password, then click Open Anyway once more.

  macOS 14 (Sonoma) or earlier:
    Right-click (or Control-click) District Lookup in Applications, choose
    Open, then click Open in the message that appears.

You only need to do this once per version. After that it opens like any
other app: a small window says it's running, and your lookup page opens in
your web browser.

The source code is public: https://github.com/drewsiegler/district-lookup
